# Pivex historical research and comparison design

The historical importers and replay can merge as offline research infrastructure after review. The experimental target is opt-in, defaults off, and is labeled **Research only — no demonstrated positive expectancy.** The historical results do not support promoting it as a trading system. [2025 baseline and entry audit](pivex-histdata-2025-study.md) · [2024 holdout and net 2R comparison](pivex-net-2r-results.md)

## Strategy interface

A `ResearchStrategy` supplies a versioned ID and an evaluator factory to the proxy replay. Each partition creates a fresh evaluator and starts flat. While flat, the evaluator receives the completed confirmation candle, tick size, copies of recent completed bars, elapsed gap intervals and the most recent accepted entry timestamp. M5 history ends at confirmation close. M15 and H1 history end at confirmation open. An optional structural level must have a timestamp at or before confirmation open. There is no next-open or future candle in the evaluator context. State may track an armed setup, but the evaluator is called only while flat, so elapsed intervals must be checked by timestamp or read from recent history after a trade.

The evaluator returns an unavailable/no-setup reason or a directional intent with stop and target. The engine stamps the confirmation and owns contiguous next-open entry, costs, position accounting, the $25 planned-risk cap, intrabar stop-first ordering and gap handling. The default evaluator retains the original trend pullback. The separately selected net 2R target calculation runs at entry. Neither mode is connected to order execution. The HistData M1 bid importer and separate paired-tick importer retain UTC normalization, hashes, quote basis and source-specific provenance. Bid candles cannot resolve ask-only touches or intrabar order.

## Candidate specifications

These rules are proposals for a new study. A completed M5 candle is represented by its opening and closing timestamps. Comparisons are strict unless stated otherwise. A tick buffer is two source ticks. Use America/New_York to construct session timestamps, then compare them in UTC. The common entry window for A, B and D is 08:30–12:00 New York time, with the next open strictly before 12:00. D cannot signal until its opening range is complete. A and B use the most recent completed gold session from 18:00 to 17:00 New York time. A holiday or unexplained gap invalidates any reference window it affects. Skip a session if the required range is incomplete. Permit one accepted entry per session for A, B and D. Positions use the shared replay exits and may remain open after the entry window.

| Family | Confirmed entry and invalidation | Stop | Target and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Previous-day breakout and retest | Long when M5 closes above the previous-session high after a close at or below it. Within the next six contiguous M5 candles, require a low at or below that high and a close back above it. A close back at or below the level before confirmation, an unknown gap or the entry cutoff cancels the setup. Mirror for a short below the previous low. | Two ticks below the long retest low or above the short retest high. | Minimum net 2R at the actual next open, bounded by the nearest level in the trade direction beyond the breakout level among highs (long) or lows (short) of the five completed sessions preceding the reference session. Freeze the boundary before confirmation. No eligible boundary means no entry. |
| B. Previous-day rejection and reversal | Short when one completed M5 candle trades strictly above the previous-session high and closes back below it. Require the very next contiguous M5 candle to close below the sweep candle's low, without trading above the sweep high. Mirror for a long after sweeping the previous low. Skip a sweep that breaches both edges. A failed next confirmation cancels the setup. | Two ticks beyond the sweep extreme. | Minimum net 2R at next open, bounded by the opposite edge of the completed prior-session range. |
| C. Trend pullback with confirmation | Keep the existing control. Long requires the latest completed H1 close above EMA20 and three increasing H1 highs and lows. Of the last three M15 candles, the middle candle closes bearish and breaks the first candle's low. The third closes bullish above the middle candle's high. M5 confirms when the preceding close is at or below that middle high and the latest close is above it. Mirror for shorts. H1/M15 must be available at confirmation open, and gaps use the current warm-up rules. Existing control has no time-of-day entry filter. | Two ticks below the lower of the pullback and reclaim lows for longs, above their higher high for shorts. | Require at least gross 2R of room at confirmation within the prior 120-hour structure lookback. Baseline sets gross 2R from confirmation close. The existing experimental variant computes minimum net 2R at next open within the same frozen boundary. Keep both configurations as controls. |
| D. Opening-range breakout and retest | Freeze the high and low of the six contiguous M5 bars from 08:30 to 09:00 New York time. Long when a later M5 close crosses above the high from at or below it. Within the next six contiguous M5 candles require a low at or below that high and a close above it. A prior close back at or below, an unknown gap or the cutoff cancels the setup. Mirror below the range low for shorts. | Two ticks outside the retest extreme, below for longs and above for shorts. | Minimum net 2R at next open, bounded by the breakout edge plus the frozen opening-range width for longs, minus it for shorts. |

The common comparison policy prices one modeled ounce at the next contiguous open. Planned risk equals adverse stop distance times the ounce quantity plus modeled spread, slippage and fees. Net reward subtracts the same round-trip charge from gross target reward. Require planned risk at or below $25 and within available equity, and net reward at least twice planned risk. Round required targets away from entry to the nearest eligible tick. Skip entries that fail geometry, session eligibility or the target boundary. These are research quantities.

**Test D first.** Its six-candle range and timestamp cutoff are directly observable in the data. It tests a different setup from the previous pullback experiment without requiring multi-day reference levels. Freeze the session, retest horizon, quantity, costs and target rules before scoring. Compare C as a control, and keep its published 2024/2025 results as prior evidence.

## Comparison and release checks

- Record the candidate version, source checksum, dataset limitations, development and holdout dates, base costs and predeclared adverse costs before viewing holdout outcomes. Reserve a later unexamined holdout. Treat 2024 and 2025 as examined for any future selection.
- Report every rejected entry and completed trade, including rejection reasons, outcome, planned risk, realized modeled P&L and costs. Report unresolved exposure separately from completed P&L. Include evaluation counts, expectancy, drawdown, profit factor and where each replay stopped. Compare variants on the same periods and observations.
- Preserve missing observations. A known closure can advance time without an invented fill. An unexplained gap with open exposure halts the replay and leaves the trade unresolved. A missing next open cancels the pending entry. Do not rank candidates solely on repeated optimization of examined periods.
- Review scope is limited to Pivex offline import, research and replay, with regression coverage of the existing entry and exit gates. Freebuff charting, SPY/QQQ research and production broker integrations are outside the diff. The experimental losses are a reason to retain the label and opt-in selection.
- Preview evidence remains limited. Netlify previews are deliberately skipped for this PR and the earlier hosted preview required authentication. A legacy Cloudflare Git integration still queues a preview build on pushes, despite the removal of the repository's Workers deployment scripts. The build for this review is cancelled at the provider. The repository's deployment settings remain as configured.
