## Reproduce with an authorized export

1. Obtain each instrument's paired historical quotes under terms permitting the intended local research. Keep the original export and its checksum outside the checkout. Record the exact source, account/feed product or export route, extraction settings, source file checksums and applicable permission in the manifest references. Review any terms affecting publication of data or derived results.
2. Normalize the authorized export to a UTF-8 CSV per instrument with header `timestamp,bid,ask`, UTC ISO 8601 timestamps with at most millisecond precision, and decimal prices. Keep ascending timestamps and original order within a millisecond. For an authorized JForex history session, obtain the two sides from the same historical tick via `getTicks` in bounded date chunks or the `readTicks` callback. Convert its epoch milliseconds to UTC ISO and serialize bid and ask with a decimal point. Use disjoint half-open chunks. For a daily BI5 export follow the provider's binary layout and independently verify its symbol-specific integer price divisor before converting. A product's advertised pip or point value alone does not establish quote precision.
3. Build the manifest using five-minute-aligned half-open `from` and `to` bounds. Choose `quoteDecimals` from the confirmed feed precision. Enter regular breaks and weekends using the provider's published local session hours and the applicable timezone, plus dated holiday exceptions. Convert each endpoint with its local date and timezone before recording it in UTC. Gold uses America/New_York and US 100 Tech uses America/Chicago in the provider's historical session reference. Compare these hours with the export and applicable holiday calendar. Include the source on each sorted, nonoverlapping closure. Unexplained absences remain missing. [Historical session reference](https://www.dukascopy.com/wiki/en/page/preview/?url=%2Fdevelopment%2Fdata-export%2Ftrading-sessions)
4. Freeze the model, sizing and split before viewing validation results. One proposed study covers March 1 through November 8, 2026 UTC with a September 1 split, subject to export availability. Each replay starts with independent equity and no carried orders or positions. Earlier candles can warm indicators and establish historical levels for a later period.
5. From `web`, run the following with the repository's supported Node version. The local CLI accepts a single instrument per run, up to 366 calendar days. It writes new output files and includes the SHA-256 of the exact paired CSV in the report.

```sh
node scripts/pivex-history.mjs manifest.json ticks.csv config.json report.json normalized.json
```

The final argument is optional. Manifest fields are `providerSymbol`, `sourceReference`, `exportReference`, `rightsReference`, `exportedAt`, `from`, `to`, `quoteDecimals` and `closedPeriods` (objects with `from`, `to`, `source`). Config fields are `from`, `splitAt`, `to`, `startingEquity`, `units`, `riskBudget`, `spreadFloor`, `roundTripSlippage`, `roundTripFees` and `costsReference`. Spread floor and slippage are positive quote-price distances. Fees are total USD per trade. Cost and size assumptions must be recorded for each instrument before validation.

## Normalization and execution

- The offline schema 2 output identifies external proxy data and preserves the original Dukascopy symbol, product type, source and usage references. The schema 1 broker-exact and synthetic imports retain their existing validation and limits.
- Each five-minute bucket uses observed ticks only. Bid and ask OHLC come from their corresponding quotes and midpoint OHLC uses the midpoint of each paired tick. The midpoint grid is half the input quote increment. The importer rejects crossed, off-grid, non-UTC and out-of-order quotes. Adjacent identical price updates at the same instant count as duplicate quote observations. Different updates at the same instant retain their source order.
- Fifteen-minute and hourly candles require three and twelve consecutive completed five-minute buckets respectively. All bucket boundaries use UTC. An observed five-minute bucket establishes at least one quote during that interval. Sparse observations within a bucket and missing source ticks inside it cannot be inferred from OHLC. A conflicting closure fails import. The report counts absent five-minute slots, separately identifying sourced closures.
- The existing EMA20 and hourly swing, 15-minute reclaim and 5-minute close-through criteria drive replay. Completed hourly observations can carry the EMA across verified closures. Missing open-market buckets reset hourly warm-up. The 15-minute and 5-minute patterns require contiguous candles. The structural bounds are the highest high and lowest low of up to 120 prior completed hourly candles from the current warm-up regime. Their observation times precede or coincide with the start of the confirmation candle.
- Entries use the contiguous next five-minute open and recompute net 2:1 and the $25 planned-risk cap there. Midpoint fills charge the greater of observed entry spread and the preset spread floor, plus declared round-trip slippage and fees. One modeled unit earns or loses one USD per dollar of quote movement. The common execution report calls the quantity `lots`. That value represents research units for proxy runs. Costs are approximate and the study does not establish liquidity, exit spread, financing, market-impact or challenge-rule compliance. Include any financing allowance in the declared model and review spread sensitivity before freezing the validation run.
- A gap-through stop uses the adverse observed open, including a reopening after a fully documented closure. A bar touching both stop and target is scored stop-first. A missing open-market interval with exposure ends that partition with unresolved P&L. Pending entries expire at gaps or partition boundaries. An open position at the end stays unresolved. Drawdown and net P&L reflect completed trades.
- The JSON contains observed and evaluated bar counts, qualified setups, trades, diagnostics, resolved-only metrics, data coverage and breakdowns by instrument, UTC session, direction and trend condition. Review diagnostics and unresolved positions alongside every performance figure.
